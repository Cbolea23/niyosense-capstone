End-User License Agreement (EULA) & Data Privacy

Republic Act No. 10173 (Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012)

1. Purpose & Decision-Support Scope

NiyoSense is an AI-assisted coconut maturity profiling system developed as an academic capstone and agricultural decision-support tool for Cocos nucifera grading (Malauhog, Buko, and Reject). It assists farmers, field managers, and plantation owners in harvest quality auditing and batch valuation.

2. Data Privacy Compliance (RA 10173)

In compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), NiyoSense collects only the minimum operational telemetry required for farm grading audits:

  • Coconut visual sample images and 5-second acoustic tap recordings
  • Assigned field manager username, device identifier, and scan timestamps
  • Bunch counts (bungkos) and maturity classification probabilities

No unrelated personal files, contacts, personal photos, or background conversations are accessed or shared with third-party advertisers.

3. Local Storage, Security & Retention

Field scans are stored locally on mobile SQLite and synchronized over encrypted HTTPS/JWT channels to the authorized farm server. Farm owners and field managers retain full ownership of their plantation records and may export or delete records at any time.

4. Limitation of Liability

AI predictions rely on lighting conditions, husk surface visibility, and acoustic tap clarity. NiyoSense provides automated grading estimates "AS IS" as a supplementary decision-support tool. Final commercial grading and settlement decisions remain the responsibility of the farm owner and field manager.
